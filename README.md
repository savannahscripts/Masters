# Masters  
**Scripts for Data Integration and Analysis of Marine Fish Occurrence Data (South Africa EEZ)**  

---

## 📂 Overview  
This repository contains R scripts used to integrate, clean, and analyse multiple marine fish datasets to assess spatial, temporal, taxonomic, and ecological patterns across South Africa’s Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ).  

The workflow progresses from data integration and standardisation through to coverage analyses, biodiversity patterns, and assemblage structure.

---

## 🔁 Workflow Structure  

Scripts are intended to be run sequentially:

### **1. Data Integration and Preparation**
- `1_1_1_MERGE_CLEAN.R` – Merge raw datasets and perform initial cleaning  
- `1_1_2_MERGE_ENV.R` – Integrate environmental and spatial data  

---

### **2. Coverage Analyses**
- `3_SPATIAL_COVERAGE.R` – Spatial sampling coverage across the EEZ  
- `4_TEMPORAL_COVERAGE.R` – Temporal sampling intensity and distribution  
- `5_DEPTH_COVERAGE.R` – Depth distribution and sampling biases  

---

### **3. Taxonomic and Biodiversity Analyses**
- `6_TAXONOMIC_COMP.R` – Taxonomic composition and resolution  
- `7_SPECIES_RICHNESS.R` – Species richness patterns and gradients  

---

### **4. Assemblage and Distribution Analyses**
- `8_ASSEMBLAGE.R` – Community structure and turnover analyses  
- `9_DISTRIBUTIONS.R` – Species distribution patterns  

---

### **5. Supporting Scripts**
- `x_STRUCTURE_RESULTS.R` – Supporting code for structuring and exporting results  

---

## 📊 Outputs  
The scripts generate figures and summary outputs used in the thesis, including:

- Sampling coverage maps  
- Temporal and depth distributions  
- Taxonomic composition plots  
- Species richness gradients  
- Assemblage structure analyses  

---

## ⚠️ Notes  
- Large raw datasets and spatial files are **not included** in this repository.  
- All analyses are based on a cleaned and integrated dataset generated through the workflow.  
- Figures can be regenerated by running the scripts in order.
